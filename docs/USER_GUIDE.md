# AILaga — User Guide

AILaga is a caregiving app that keeps every record **on this phone only**.
No account, no internet needed. It helps you record medications,
measurements, appointments, and care notes — and share clear summaries
with family or the next caregiver.

> **AILaga is not a medical device.** Consult a healthcare professional
> for medical advice.

---

## 1. Getting started

The first time you open the app, it asks you to add a **care recipient**
— the person you are caring for (name, date of birth, allergies,
important notes, emergency info). After that, every screen works for
that person.

You can edit the profile later from **Settings → Care recipient profile**.

## 2. Finding your way

The bar at the bottom has five items:

| Tab | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Today** | The day's medications, vital signs, appointments, and tasks |
| **Reports** | Daily care brief, caregiver handover, PDF reports |
| **Speak** (big middle button) | Record or type a care update |
| **Ask** | Ask questions about the saved records |
| **Settings** | Notifications, emergency, privacy, helper, data |

The top of every main screen shows:

- **On phone** (left, shield badge) — a reminder that all data stays
  on this phone.
- **SOS** (right, red) — opens the emergency screen. It is always one
  tap away, on every screen.

## 3. Recording care (the main job)

### Today screen

- **Medications** — tap **Taken** or **Skip** next to a pill when it is
  given. Overdue pills turn red so they cannot be missed.
- **Recent vitals** — tap **Add** to record blood pressure, pulse,
  temperature, weight, or blood sugar.
- **Appointments** and **Care notes** — tap an item to see details, or
  use the **+** buttons on each section.

### Speak (record an update)

Tap the big **Speak** button, then either:

1. **Record** — hold the mic and talk naturally, e.g.
   *"Lola took Metformin, BP 130 over 80."* (Recording stops by itself
   after 30 seconds.)
2. **Type** — write the same kind of sentence in the box and tap
   **Process**.
3. **Take a photo** — of a prescription, medicine label, or monitor
   reading.

The phone then turns your words or photo into **cards**. Nothing is
saved yet — you always review first.

### Review tray (always check before saving)

Each card shows what the phone heard and a flag:

- **Sure** (green ✔) — the phone is confident.
- **Check** (orange ⚠) — read carefully; something may be wrong.

On each card you can **Edit** or **Discard**. Tap **Confirm all** to
save. Only what you confirm is written to the record.

> The "Phone helper" only suggests. **You always decide.**

## 4. Asking questions (Ask)

Tap **Ask** and type a question, or tap a suggestion such as
*"Did she take pills today?"* or *"What was the last BP?"*

It reads only the records on this phone. If the Phone helper is off,
you still get simple answers.

## 5. Reports (share with family or the next caregiver)

- **Daily Care Brief** — the day's medications, vitals, notes, and
  items that still need attention. Use the arrows to change the day.
- **Caregiver Handover** — a ready-to-send summary for the next
  caregiver. Tap **Copy** or **Share** to send it by any app.
- **Reports** — a PDF report you can share or print for doctors.

> Sharing sends the report outside the app — choose who receives it.

## 6. Emergency (SOS)

Tap the red **SOS** button on any screen (or the big red button on the
emergency screen):

1. A short countdown starts — tap **Cancel** if pressed by accident.
2. When it finishes, the phone **opens the dialer on 911** so you can
   call for help.
3. Below, your **trusted contacts** are listed with big **Call** and
   **Text** buttons for each person.

Also in **Settings**:

- **Shake for SOS** — shake the phone quickly to open the emergency
  screen (works while the app is open). On by default.
- **Fall detection** — after a hard fall, the app asks "Are you okay?"
  and offers to get help if you don't respond.

Set up family contacts under **Settings → Family contacts**.

## 7. Settings

| Item | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Notifications** | Shows On/Off honestly; tap to open the phone's settings |
| **Reminder timing** | Pills remind when due; appointments 30 minutes before |
| **Emergency number** | 911 — opens the phone dialer |
| **Shake for SOS / Fall detection** | Sensor-based emergency shortcuts |
| **Phone helper** | Download, check, or remove the on-device helper that understands speech |
| **On my phone** | Proof that data stays local — shows real network counters for this app |
| **Health Connect** | Import pulse/BP/etc. from Android health apps, with a preview before saving |
| **Camera pulse** | Estimate pulse by covering the camera with a fingertip (an estimate, not a medical reading) |
| **Delete all data** | Erases every record on this phone — asks twice, cannot be undone |
| **Version** | Shows the real app version |

## 8. Privacy & honesty — what the app will never do

- Upload your data. Everything lives on this phone; the **On phone**
  badge and the *On my phone* panel show proof.
- Call or text anyone by itself — it only **opens the dialer** so *you*
  press send.
- Save an AI suggestion without your confirmation.
- Pretend an appointment happened or a pill was taken — summaries only
  report what was actually recorded.

## 9. Tips for everyday use

- Big buttons and large text are on purpose — nothing needs precise
  tapping.
- Every action has an **icon and a word** — look for the word.
- If something looks wrong, use **Edit** or **Discard** before
  confirming — nothing is final until you say so.
- No internet needed anywhere in the app, ever.
